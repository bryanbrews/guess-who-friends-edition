How this number was built

Not an appraisal. Estimates combine public Gutachterausschuss market-report averages (from the notarized-sale register, the Kaufpreissammlung) with listing asking prices discounted to transaction level, then adjust for size, age, condition and micro-location. Data vintage: —. For a binding valuation, consult the local Gutachterausschuss or a certified appraiser.
